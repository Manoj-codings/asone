our story

asone was created with one belief — clothing should speak through restraint. we strip away the noise, the logos, the excess, and leave only what matters: the cut, the fabric, the feeling on your skin. every piece is designed to make you feel complete — as one.

the founders

srinivas

co-founder & creative director

nivas brings a decade of design experience from luxury fashion houses. his vision for asone is rooted in indian minimalism and the belief that less is always more.

bala ganapathy

co-founder & head of craft

bala oversees every stitch, every fabric choice. with roots in sustainable textile engineering, she ensures every asone piece is crafted with intention and care.
